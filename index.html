Grade 9 — keep every door open

No university looks at Grade 9 marks. Your only job: stay on the course track that leads to Grade 12 U-level courses.

Take this year

  • ENG1D English (academic / de-streamed) — starts the chain to ENG4U, required everywhere
  • MTH1W Math (de-streamed) — starts the chain to Grade 12 math and calculus
  • SNC1W Science — starts the chain to Grade 12 Biology, Chemistry, Physics

One habit that pays off later

  • Pick at least one arts/humanities course you actually like — McMaster Health Sci requires a non-math, non-science Grade 12 course, and it's easier if you've stayed in the habit.
⚠ Course codes reflect Ontario's current curriculum — confirm exact codes with your guidance counselor, as the Ministry occasionally renames them.

Grade 10 — the chain continues

These three academic courses are the prerequisites for the Grade 11 courses that matter. Dropping to applied level here closes the U-level path.

Take this year

  • ENG2D English (academic) → leads to ENG3U → ENG4U
  • MPM2D Principles of Mathematics (academic) → leads to MCR3U → MHF4U / MCV4U / MDM4U
  • SNC2D Science (academic) → the single gateway to Grade 11 Biology, Chemistry AND Physics
⚠ Confirm course codes and prerequisites with your guidance counselor — chains can vary slightly by school board.

Grade 11 — the year that quietly decides everything

Universities mostly look at Grade 12 marks — but every Grade 12 course has a Grade 11 prerequisite. What you pick now determines what you can pick next year.

To keep all five flagship pre-med programs open next year, take:

  • ENG3U English → unlocks ENG4U (required by every program)
  • SBI3U Biology → unlocks SBI4U (required: McMaster, Western ×2, Queen's)
  • SCH3U Chemistry → unlocks SCH4U (required: McMaster, Western Med Sci, Queen's)
  • MCR3U Functions → unlocks ALL Grade 12 math, including Calculus
  • SPH3U Physics (optional) → unlocks SPH4U, recommended at Western Med Sci

The trap to avoid

  • Skipping MCR3U (Functions) means no Calculus next year — which closes Western Medical Sciences and U of T Life Sciences permanently for direct entry. It's the most door-closing single decision in high school. Tap through to the Grade 12 checker to see it live.
⚠ Confirm prerequisites with your guidance counselor before finalizing your timetable.

Undergraduate — med school requirements

Canadian med schools differ a lot more than people assume — some require specific courses, some require almost none. We're verifying each school against the official AFMC guide; verified entries appear below as we finish them.

Western (Schulich School of Medicine), Ontario

  • Course prerequisites: none reported — admission is based on GPA, MCAT and the interview, not specific undergrad courses
  • MCAT: required, written within 5 years; your most recent score is used
